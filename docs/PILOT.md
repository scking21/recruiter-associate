## First-use handoff

Open [Roletrace](https://aiworthusing.com/agent-index/recruiter-associate) and use its hosted setup. Begin with: “Help me review one work-sample excerpt against one job criterion.” Answer the agent's authorization and source questions before sharing evidence. A real applicant excerpt is not needed just to inspect the listing or watch the labeled demo.

The first useful result should contain a cited finding, the specific gap that remains unknown, and a question the hiring owner can answer. Check the citation against the excerpt. In an owner-authorized shared conversation, have the actual collaborator contribute any genuine clarification, then ask: “Show what changed in the review and why; preserve the previous version.” Check that unrelated findings did not change. Do not manufacture a correction merely to complete the script.

Record onboarding failures separately from usefulness: could the user finish setup, get the first useful result, understand it, and continue with their collaborator? Fix the first observed failure before expanding scope. A hosted install may report usage automatically; do not run fictional rehearsals there to inflate the leaderboard.

## Prerequisites

The owner confirms permission to process the minimized work-sample evidence, allowed sources, and the trusted room and collaborator scope. Use one role and owner-stated criterion initially. Keep private artifacts outside tracked source; agree handling and deletion expectations with participants before starting.

## Exercise

1. Have the owner prepare the same bounded evidence review using their usual method. Record their active minutes and the review questions produced.
2. Start the agent with “Help me review one work-sample excerpt against one job criterion.” Record intake preparation time separately from agent wait time. The agent assigns internal IDs; the owner supplies the actual criterion, authority assertions, authorized source, and minimized excerpt.
3. Check every cited finding against the permitted excerpt. Mark unsupported claims, missing evidence, attribution mistakes, and manual corrections. Validation passing alone does not establish factual accuracy.
4. Have the authorized collaborator supply a genuine correction or clarification if one exists. If none exists, record that the correction case was not exercised. Do not invent real evidence for the test.
5. Check that the revised review identifies the changed evidence and affected finding, preserves the earlier version, and keeps unaffected findings intact. The owner retains the hiring decision; no candidate contact or automated disposition occurs.
6. Ask both participants whether they would choose the workflow for another review, and why. Do not equate an install or token count with that answer.

## Private measurement sheet

Record baseline active minutes; agent intake minutes; review/correction minutes; wait time; number of findings checked; supported citations divided by citations checked; unsupported claims; unresolved questions; corrections; and willingness to reuse. Leave untested measures blank with a reason. Include evidence preparation in total human effort.

Stop and reassess if authorization or room scope is uncertain, private evidence crosses a boundary, a hiring decision is automated, or an unsupported claim remains after review. The usefulness hypothesis fails this small test if total human effort is no lower than the baseline or the participants would not reuse it. One successful pilot is preliminary evidence, not a general accuracy or fairness claim.
