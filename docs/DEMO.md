# 75 second demo recording plan

Record after the real owner and collaborator flow works. This script is preparation, not a finished video. Use permissioned, minimized evidence and conceal applicant identifiers, credentials, gateway pairing URLs and private account details. A synthetic walkthrough must say synthetic and cannot establish real use.

| Time | Show | Say or demonstrate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0 to 10 seconds | Cover, then the real startup recruiting task | State the recurring job and who owns hiring decisions. |
| 10 to 25 seconds | Owner provides agreed job criteria and minimized evidence in the dedicated OpenClaw agent | Show what the agent is authorized to review. |
| 25 to 45 seconds | Agent prepares cited findings; validation runs | Show one citation and one missing fact. The agent does the evidence preparation. |
| 45 to 60 seconds | The actual collaborator joins with a distinct identity and asks a useful follow up | Show the shared session and real participant attribution. |
| 60 to 75 seconds | Human reviews the result, then public installation/listing | Explain the saved work and demonstrate where others install it. |

Before submission, verify video duration is at least 60 seconds, publish with public or unlisted access as intended, and play the actual watch URL without the owner's session. Save the verified URL in HACKATHON.md. A local render, upload, draft or scheduled video is not publication. Do not show sample counters as real leaderboard results.
