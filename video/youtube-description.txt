TITLE: Roletrace | Every hiring finding, traced to its source | OpenClaw demo

Roletrace prepares cited, job-related evidence reviews for a human hiring owner.

This 94-second controlled demonstration shows a fictional minimized evidence bundle, the real-mode guarded prompt, a saved agent response, fresh offline validation, and a replay of excerpts from a saved shared-session simulation in which a simulated manager withdraws a claim and the agent marks the finding unverified. No real applicants or real collaborators appear. Human hiring decisions and candidate communication stay with people.

Agent Index: https://aiworthusing.com/agent-index/recruiter-associate
MIT source and installation: https://github.com/scking21/roletrace

Motion graphics and captions were built in Remotion; narration is ElevenLabs Brian (synthetic). The saved recruiter responses came from the project's NVIDIA GLM 5.3 runtime.

The validation checks structure and bounded rules, not factual truth, fairness, legal compliance, or hiring fitness.
